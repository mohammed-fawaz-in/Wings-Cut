Where the sample photos in this folder came from
==================================================

These are PLACEHOLDER photos so the site doesn't look empty while you collect
your own work. They were pulled from public GitHub template repositories that
are published under the MIT licence:

  - jaugustodev/portfolio-photographer      (city / travel / night shots)
  - CristhGunners/Att                       (landscapes)
  - NN-Bayat/Photography-AlinaLee           (portraits, one couple shot)
  - pasha804/event-organization-agency-website  (events, one wedding dress shot)
  - tirthuix/Wedding-Planner-Website        (wedding venues and couples)
  - dyCodes/EventPlanner                    (event venues and decor)

A heads-up: the MIT licence covers each repo's code. I couldn't confirm where
the photos inside them originally came from (they look like typical Unsplash /
Pexels style stock). Before the site goes public for real clients, replace
them with your own work. A studio site should show your photos anyway.

How to swap them
----------------
Delete the sample files in a folder and drop your own in. The page picks up
whatever is in images/photos/<category>/ automatically, no code change needed.
